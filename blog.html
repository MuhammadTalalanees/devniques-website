What Is MDM?

MDM stands for mobile device management. It is the software that lets a business control and secure all its work devices — phones, tablets, laptops — from one dashboard. With MDM you can enroll devices, enforce passwords and security policies, install apps, track devices, and wipe a lost or stolen device remotely. For a business with staff on the move, MDM is how IT stays in control.
