Devniques MDM — Mobile Device Management

Devniques MDM gives a business total control of its device fleet. It helps manage and secure phones, tablets, laptops and POS devices from one console — with enrollment, policies, apps and remote lock/wipe. Instead of managing every device by hand, a company enrolls its devices, applies policies, and monitors activity remotely.

Devniques MDM in one line: One Platform. Total Device Control. Enterprise-Grade Security. A business that wants to see MDM in action can book a demo at devniques.com/mdm-demo/.
